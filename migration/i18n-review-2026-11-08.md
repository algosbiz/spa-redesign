# Language review of the staging site: mistakes found and fixed (8 Nov 2026)

The review covered the staging site in Japanese (/jp/), Korean (/kr/), Chinese (/cn/) and Hindi (/in/), on branch `staging` at 9bcbbf7.

**How it was checked:**
- **Text:** all 45 sitemap pages were fetched in each of the four languages (180 pages, about 28,500 text blocks), looking for text with none of the language's characters in it.
- **Phones:** 11 main pages in each language were loaded at 390px wide (44 loads), checked for overlapping text and for anything wider than the screen.
- **Code:** the translation files, the 404 route and the English pages were compared with the live site.

The fixes are on branch `fix/i18n-review`, which goes into `staging`.

## Checked and fine

- **Same images, prices and sections as English:** across all 35 translated pages in all four languages (140 page pairs), every image is identical (1,304 images on the English pages), so the 2–3 Nov photo update is everywhere. Every section count matches, and every price matches apart from #7.
- **Staging's English matches the live site:** every image and price on all 35 pages is identical (8 Nov).
- **Translations complete:** about 5,000 texts in each language, none empty.
- **No English left in the page text** in any language, apart from #1 and #6. The other Latin text is names, addresses, prices and reviewers' names, as intended.
- **Descriptions translated:** every page description is in its language. Every Japanese and Hindi title contains its language.
- **Phones:** 44 page loads, with no overlapping text and nothing wider than the screen in any language.
- **Language switcher:** it keeps you on the same page in the other language (e.g. /jp/seminyak/balinese-massage/ → /kr/seminyak/balinese-massage/).
- **Structured data:** the business description is in the page's language, with the same business @id in every language.
- **English unchanged:** all 487 English headings on staging match the live site, including the 4 Nov changes. Merging staging will not undo anything on the English site.
- **No links to missing pages:** translated pages never link to the untranslated guides. Their guide links go to the English articles.

## As designed (not mistakes)

- **noindex and no hreflang:** language pages carry `noindex, nofollow` and no hreflang links while they are in preview (`I18N_PREVIEW_LOCALES`). They switch on per language when it is added to `activeLocales` in `src/i18n/config.ts`, which also adds it to the sitemap.
- **Guide pages 404 in other languages:** the blog articles live in the database and are not translated yet.
- **Names in Latin letters:** service and place names, the brand, addresses and prices stay in Latin letters (owner, 3 Nov 2026).
- **WebSite structured data says "en":** it describes the whole site, so it deliberately stays the same in every language (`src/components/seo/JsonLd.tsx`).

2. Fix #6 (the English 404 for addresses that match no page).
3. Translate the guides, or decide that they launch later.
4. Add the language to `activeLocales`, then check its hreflang links, the sitemap and Search Console.
5. Optional SEO idea: Japanese, Korean and Chinese searchers often type the treatment in their own script (e.g. バリニーズマッサージ). Adding it once beside the Latin name in titles could help. That is an exception to the 3 Nov naming rule, so it is the owner's call.
